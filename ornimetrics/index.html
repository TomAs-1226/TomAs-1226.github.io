Ornimetrics

A bird feeder that knows who's eating. It names the species, feeds each one its own portion, spots birds that need help and learns its regulars, all on a Raspberry Pi.

Why

The end of the scramble

An ordinary tray is first come, first served: bullies crowd out timid birds, squirrels empty it, and leftover seed rots. Ornimetrics decides who eats.

Watchthe camera runs the detector continuously
Recognisebird, squirrel, person or dog, then the species

Parts tour

Four parts

Dispensing mechanism. A rotating disk with two pockets meters the seed out in exact amounts, and an incline under it pours the portion gently onto the tray. This is the mechanism behind the patent.

Tray. Shaped so birds can grip the edge while they eat their portion. Five 3 mm screws lock the dispensing mechanism to it, carrying the weight of the seed and the birds.

Feed storage. The hopper on top holds the seed and feeds the disk below it by gravity.

Camera housing. Holds the camera right at the tray, under the hopper, so every bird that lands is in frame.

All four modelled and animated in Spline. Drag to turn them.
