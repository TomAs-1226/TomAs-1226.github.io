Built

Off to the fab

Six copper layers, zero design-rule errors, fabricated in August 2026 and assembled by hand.

The hard part

Why six layers

Nothing fits between the processor's pads, so all 128 pins escape outward on the top layer. Four layers ran out of room; six didn't.
